## Attribution

Visible credits are generated from `src/data/provenance.json` in the footer's Photo credits dialog. Credits include the recorded source title, author, licence and links. Verify each licence's attribution/share-alike requirements and suitability for commercial travel-agency marketing before launch. Image cropping/resizing should be acknowledged as shown in the dialog.

The strict image script cannot identify the depicted location from pixels. This review is the human check; re-review any image that is replaced or recropped substantially.
